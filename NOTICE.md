# Third-Party Data & Audio Attributions (NOTICE)

FuLuoXiaoJie (弗洛小姐) builds its offline Simplified Mandarin DAG, dictionary, stroke-order graphics, frequency corpora, and syllable audio from open-source and open-access linguistic resources:

1. **Make Me a Hanzi** (`skishore/makemeahanzi`)
   - Character dictionary, IDS decompositions, and etymology hints derived from Unihan / CJKlib (LGPL / Unicode).
   - Stroke vector paths and medians (`graphics.txt`) derived from Arphic PL KaitiM GB and Arphic PL UKai fonts under the Arphic Public License.
   - Source: https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi

2. **CJKVI IDS** (`cjkvi/cjkvi-ids`)
   - Ideographic Description Sequences based on the CHISE IDS database (GPLv2 / CHISE terms).
   - Source: https://github.com/cjkvi/cjkvi-ids

3. **CC-CEDICT**
   - Chinese-English dictionary and multi-character vocabulary entries licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0).
   - Source: https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict

4. **SUBTLEX-CH** (Cai, Q., & Brysbaert, M., 2010)
   - Chinese character and word frequencies based on film subtitles (PLoS ONE, CC BY).
   - Reference: Cai, Q., & Brysbaert, M. (2010). SUBTLEX-CH: Chinese Word and Character Frequencies Based on Film Subtitles. *PLoS ONE*, 5(6), e10729.

5. **Jun Da Modern Chinese Character Frequency List** (Da, J., 2004)
   - Modern Chinese written corpus character frequencies (via `nieldlr/hanzi`).

6. **HSK 3.0 Character List** (`ivankra/hsk30`)
   - Cleaned HSK 3.0 character level mappings (MIT).
   - Source: https://github.com/ivankra/hsk30

7. **Mandarin Syllable Audio**
   - Primary pack: `davinfifield/mp3-chinese-pinyin-sound` — Public Domain (Unlicense).
   - Supplemental syllables: `hugolpz/audio-cmn` (Chen Wang / SWAC Recorder curation) — Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA).
